# Unleashed 1000 — 2026-09-10

## Player contract

- `RunModifiers.unleashedMode` is an explicit Roster-screen toggle.
- Enabled: authored wave cadence is 8× and the live-enemy cap is 1,000.
- Disabled: the original 190-enemy cap and ordinary visual budgets remain unchanged.
- Unleashed overrides the spawn-rate portion of 2× and 4× modes rather than multiplying with them. Their non-spawn effects, such as 2× Mode's hp bump, still apply.

## Performance contract

- Simulation owns the complete swarm, but rendering filters to the camera before sorting or drawing rigs.
- Spatial-grid separation resolves each pair once, not once from each actor's side.
- The enemy update prefilters movable breakables once per frame.
- At 300+ enemies, decorative particles and damage-number popups use smaller fixed budgets.
- Enemy-fired projectiles stop spawning at the Unleashed projectile budget; player projectiles are never rejected by that budget.
- Enemy-created cosmetic beams and rings use a separate budget; player weapon effects remain untouched.
- Endless mode raises its spawn-rate ceiling from 3.2/s to 12/s only while Unleashed is active.

## Regression coverage

- A saturated normal run remains capped at 190.
- A saturated Unleashed run reaches and remains capped at 1,000 across subsequent simulation steps.
- Combined 2× + 4× + Unleashed produces 8× cadence, not 64×.
- Persistence accepts only a literal boolean `true`.
